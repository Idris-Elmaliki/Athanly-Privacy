Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

Athanly is an ad-free app for prayer times, Athan notifications and Qibla direction. It is built by an independent developer, Idris Elmaliki. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Ads
None

Information the app uses

  • Location. With your permission, Athanly reads your device's location to calculate prayer times for where you are and to point you toward the Qibla. You can deny or revoke this permission at any time in your device settings.
  • Notifications. Athan notifications are scheduled and shown entirely on your device. No notification data is sent to any server.
  • Saved prayer times. The current day's prayer times are stored on your device so the app stays fast and works when your connection drops. This data never leaves your device.

Athanly does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it does not require you to create an account.

Services the app relies on

To calculate prayer times, the app sends your location coordinates to a third-party prayer-times API. The coordinates are used only to return the times for your area.

How information is used

Information is used only to show prayer times, find the Qibla direction and send Athan notifications. Athanly has no ads, does not sell your data, and does not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Keeping or deleting your data

Saved prayer times are replaced each day. Uninstalling Athanly removes everything the app stored on your device. You can also clear the app's data at any time from your device's app settings.

Children

Athanly is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Connections to the prayer-times API use encrypted HTTPS. Data stored on your device is protected by Android's app sandbox.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
